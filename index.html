The grooming

Cuts shaped to your dog — not a template.

Whether it’s a doodle that mats in a week, a double-coated shepherd, or a nervous rescue on their first-ever visit, our groomers read the coat and the dog before the clippers ever come out.

Owners tell us their pups come home looking like themselves again — not shaved down to start over. It’s the difference between a groomer who’s watching the clock and one who genuinely loves the work.

  • Breed-specific styling for coats of every length and texture
  • Patient, calm handling — great for puppies and anxious pups
  • Friendly with the breeds other groomers turn away

Bath & spa

Clean coats, calm dogs, happy homes.

A great bath is more than soap and water. We match the products to your dog’s skin — including switching things up for allergies and sensitivities — so the clean lasts and the itch doesn’t.

Warm baths, thorough drying, nails, ears and de-shedding, all at a pace that keeps even the wiggliest pup relaxed. Regulars say their dogs come home sparkling clean and smelling wonderful.

  • Gentle, warm baths with products picked for your dog’s skin
  • Nail trims and de-shedding to keep coats healthy between visits
  • Adjusted for allergies, sensitive skin and senior dogs
